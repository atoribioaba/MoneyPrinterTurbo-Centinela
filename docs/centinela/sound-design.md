# F22 · Sound Design

Version: `sound-design-v0.1`.

F22 creates a narrative sound-design cue sheet only. It selects no asset,
downloads no audio and claims no licence.

Every cue starts with:
`LICENCIA_NO_VERIFICADA`, `publication_eligible=false`.

The design is explicitly non-diegetic. F22 forbids fabricated diegetic sound in
the vacuum of space. Real-world ambience may be selected later only with
appropriate scene context and provenance.

The climax may receive one restrained non-diegetic accent. Human asset
selection remains mandatory.
