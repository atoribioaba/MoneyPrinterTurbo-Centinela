# F21 · Transition Director

Version: `transition-director-v0.1`.

F21 converts the explicit F8 transition intent into restrained concrete
transition specs. It never invents transitions for spectacle.

Allowed V0.1 transitions:
- CUT;
- SOFT_DISSOLVE;
- FADE_TO_BLACK.

Durations are capped at 0.40 s. Placeholder pairs are planned but remain
execution-disabled until media exists.
