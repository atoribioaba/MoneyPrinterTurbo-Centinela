## Story skeleton

### 1. Introduction — daylight anchor

Narrative intent: establish place, light and observational perspective before the transition begins.

Visual requirement: relevant real daylight/Sun/landscape material. No generic space footage.

Science rule: if the Sun itself is discussed numerically or geometrically, every claim must have FactLock support.

### 2. Development — sunset

Narrative intent: show the Sun approaching/disappearing at the horizon and the color/light transition.

Primary candidate material: the verified own vertical sunset clip above, subject to local review.

Avoid claiming a precise solar azimuth/time/location from filename or appearance alone.

### 3. Climax — twilight transformation

Narrative intent: the sky changes regime; exposure/color/contrast move from terrestrial sunset to astronomical twilight atmosphere.

Visual requirement: continuity with the observed landscape. Do not insert unrelated galaxy/nebula footage just to increase spectacle.

If civil/nautical/astronomical twilight times are mentioned, calculate them for the actual location/date and record them in FactLock.

### 4. Resolution — Moon reveal

Narrative intent: introduce the Moon as the next observational subject after sunset.

Visual requirement: real Moon material or scientifically valid representation appropriate to the actual claim. Phase, position, constellation and timing must match the chosen date/location if asserted.

No strong object identity may be inferred from generic lexical similarity alone.

### 5. Epilogue — observer continuity

Narrative intent: close on the project identity: observe, document, understand and share the sky.

Visual requirement: calm, coherent final shot rather than a random cosmic montage.
