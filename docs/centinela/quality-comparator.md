# F28 · Quality Comparator

Version: `quality-comparator-v0.1`.

F28 is the deterministic arbitration layer between the F9 baseline and future
enhancement candidates.

Rules:

- placeholders are not comparable;
- failed source analysis is blocked;
- an adequate baseline remains the default;
- a F26 upscale candidate never becomes the winner without explicit A/B
  evidence and human astronomy-fidelity review;
- F28 does not analyze new frames or modify media.
