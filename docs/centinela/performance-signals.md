# F33 · Content Performance Signals

Version: `performance-signals-v0.1`.

F33 does not manufacture a universal "quality score". It derives descriptive
signals only inside compatible platform cohorts.

Current signals:

- views, when available;
- total observed interactions from mapped like/comment/share/save counts;
- interactions per view;
- within-platform percentiles when the cohort has at least five comparable
  content items.

No cross-platform ranking, composite score or causal statement is produced.
